📊 YOUR EXECUTIVE READOUT

How your communication strength shows up—and where it can work against you.

01 — YOUR NATURAL ADVANTAGE
You make complexity usable.
Your instinct is to take complex ideas, competing perspectives and unclear conversations—and turn them into something people can understand and act on.
What this gives you:
  • The ability to communicate effectively across different audiences, situations and levels of complexity.
02 — HOW YOU ARE EXPERIENCED
The person who makes things clearer.
Others are likely to experience you as composed, capable and easy to follow. You can move between strategy and empathy, structure and spontaneity without losing your message.
The perception to watch
Your consistency can also create distance. Because you often appear in control, people may not always see your uncertainty, emotion or personal investment behind the message.
03 — THE STRENGTH PARADOX
Your greatest strength can also become your blind spot.
Your versatility allows you to adapt to almost any communication situation. But when you can do everything well, you may not always make your own point of view, passion or distinctive edge visible enough.
The risk is not poor communication. The risk is becoming so adaptable that others see your capability—but not always what you stand for.
04 — YOUR NEXT LEVEL
Move from versatility to deliberate distinction.
You don't need to improve everything. Your greatest opportunity is to choose one communication capability or high-stakes context where you want to become unmistakably exceptional.
Your highest-ROI move: Choose one arena where you want your communication to become your signature advantage—and practise it deliberately.
