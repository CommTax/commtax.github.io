Frequently Asked Questions

How does the impact test work?

Choose between "Introduce Yourself" or "Current Project" prompts. Then either record a 30-second audio or type a 60-second response. Our AI analyzes your communication style and reveals your blind spots.

Why is the recording only 30 seconds?

Because we want to capture how you naturally communicate without turning the diagnostic into a long assessment. Thirty seconds is enough to reveal useful signals while keeping the experience quick.

Is my voice data kept private?

Yes. Voice recordings are processed securely, never sold to third parties, and stored only to track your personal progress within your secure profile.
