Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Unspoken?

The Unspoken is a platform that helps you understand your communication persona and provides a personalized path to improve how you communicate, lead, and influence.

Who is The Unspoken for?

For ambitious professionals who want to close the gap between what they mean and what others experience.

What do I get from the free persona assessment?

You get your Communication Persona, key strengths, blind spots, and a clear next step for improvement.

How long does the assessment take?

About 5–10 minutes for the free version, and up to 20 minutes for the complete version.

What is a Communication Persona?

It's your unique pattern of communication — how you naturally express yourself and how others perceive you.

Do I need to complete lessons every day?

No. You set your own pace, but we recommend small daily actions for best results.

Is The Unspoken only about communication?

It starts with communication, but expands into leadership, confidence, influence, and career growth.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings.

Is there a community?

Yes, members get access to a community of peers, challenges, and expert insights.
