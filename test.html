Frequently Asked Questions

How does the impact test work?

Choose between "Introduce Yourself" or "Current Project" prompts. Then either record a 45-second audio or type a 60-second response. Our AI analyzes your communication style and reveals your blind spots.

Why is the recording only 45 seconds?

Research shows that 45 seconds is the optimal length to identify communication patterns while keeping the test quick and accessible. You can also type your response if you prefer.

Is my voice data kept private?

Yes. Voice recordings are processed securely, never sold to third parties, and stored only to track your personal progress within your secure profile.
