Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 60-second impact test work?

You record a short audio snippet answering a prompt. Our AI analyzes cadence, structure, clarity, and filler word density to give you an immediate report on your primary communication pattern.

Is my voice data kept private?

Yes. Voice recordings are processed securely, never sold to third parties, and stored only to track your personal progress within your secure profile.

How quickly can I expect results?

Most users notice a conscious shift in their meeting structure within 3 to 5 days of practicing 5-minute daily modules.
