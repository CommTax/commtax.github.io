Practice Exercises

5 Exercises

Start practicing immediately with these exercises:

The 1-Minute Clarity Drill
Goal: Improve your message clarity
Instructions:
  1. Before every meeting, write down your ONE message
  2. Keep it to ONE sentence
  3. Read it out loud before the meeting
Duration: 1 minute  |  Frequency: Daily, before each meeting
Pro Tip: Share your message with someone before the meeting to test if it's clear.
The Headline-First Drill
Goal: Improve your logical flow
Instructions:
  1. For one week, start every email with your main point
  2. No context, no preamble
  3. Support with evidence in the next sentences
Duration: 2 minutes per email  |  Frequency: 7 days challenge
The Completeness-Check Drill
Goal: Improve your communication completeness
Instructions:
  1. Before ending any conversation, ask: "Is there anything I haven't covered?"
  2. Wait for their response
  3. Address any gaps immediately
Duration: 1 minute  |  Frequency: After every conversation
The 60-Second Pitch Drill
Goal: Improve your value articulation
Instructions:
  1. Practice your 60-second value pitch every morning
  2. Record yourself and listen back
  3. Refine until it's compelling
Duration: 60 seconds  |  Frequency: Daily
The Audience Mapping Drill
Goal: Improve your audience awareness
Instructions:
  1. Before any conversation, write down: Who am I speaking to?
  2. What do they care about?
  3. What's their context?
Duration: 3 minutes  |  Frequency: Before each meeting

Key Insight

Start with Exercise 1. It takes 5 minutes and delivers immediate improvement.
