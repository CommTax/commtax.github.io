✦ Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you start practising.

What exactly is The Unspoken?
The Unspoken is a communication-training platform. You practise real interview and workplace answers out loud (or by typing), and we show you exactly where your point got lost — structure, clarity, filler words, delivery — then give you drills to fix it.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. The interactive sandbox on this page is a free demo — no signup required. You can also start a 4-day free trial on any plan. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.
What's the difference between Sprint and Practice Pass?
Sprint is a 4-week focused programme for a specific outcome — cracking an interview, nailing a GD, or landing a specific offer.

Practice Pass is ongoing. You practise whenever you want, across as many scenarios as you like, and keep sharpening your pattern over time.
How is feedback generated?
Your response is transcribed (voice) or read directly (text), then analysed by our engine across four dimensions: Structure, Clarity, Conciseness, and Delivery. You get a pattern name, a diagnosis of what got lost, and one specific thing to change next time. No generic advice.
Do I need to record my voice, or can I type?
Both work. Voice is the closest to how you'll actually be judged in an interview, and it captures filler words, pacing, and pauses. Typing is a good fallback if you're somewhere you can't speak — you'll still get structure and conciseness feedback.
Who is this for?
Anyone who has to speak under pressure and needs to be understood: students preparing for placements, freshers and early-career professionals preparing for interviews, and mid-to-senior professionals preparing for pitches, reviews, or high-stakes conversations.
