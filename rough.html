Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the Single Sprint and The Practice Pass?

The Single Sprint is a fixed 4-week program for one specific cohort (e.g., Interview Voice Fix). The Practice Pass gives you access to all cohorts, ongoing, with the ability to switch anytime.

What happens after the 4-week Sprint ends?

The program ends. You can rejoin the same Sprint, pick a new one, or move to The Practice Pass for ongoing access.

What's the Day-Before Boost?

It's a warm-up session available to Practice Pass members before any real interview or meeting — available whenever you need it.

Can I switch cohorts with The Practice Pass?

Yes — switch anytime at no extra cost. You're not locked into one role or context.
